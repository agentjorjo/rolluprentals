# Roll Up Rentals Website

A complete, professional website for Roll Up Rentals - a dumpster, storage container, and mobile office rental company based in Los Angeles with 13+ years experience.

## Overview

This website is designed to generate phone calls and leads from contractors and construction companies in the LA area. It features:

- **6 Pages**: Home, Dumpster Rentals, Storage Containers, Mobile Offices, About Us, and Contact
- **Contractor-Focused Design**: Professional, trustworthy appearance that appeals to construction professionals
- **Prominent CTAs**: Clear calls-to-action like "CALL NOW FOR PRICING" and "GET A QUOTE"
- **Phone Number Visibility**: Phone number highlighted throughout the site for immediate contact
- **Experience Emphasis**: Highlights 13+ years of service and local LA expertise
- **Responsive Design**: Works on all devices from mobile to desktop

## Pages

1. **index.html** - Home page with hero section, features, services overview, and CTA
2. **dumpster-rentals.html** - Detailed dumpster rental services, sizes, and pricing
3. **storage-containers.html** - Storage container options, features, and pricing
4. **mobile-offices.html** - Mobile office rentals, specifications, and benefits
5. **about.html** - Company history, team, values, and service area
6. **contact.html** - Contact information, quote request form, and service selection

## Customization

To customize this website for your specific business:

1. **Update Contact Information**: Replace phone number, email, and service areas
2. **Modify Pricing**: Update rate tables in dumpster-rentals.html, storage-containers.html, and mobile-offices.html
3. **Change Images**: Replace placeholder images with actual photos of your equipment and team
4. **Update Service Areas**: Modify the service area information in about.html and contact.html
5. **Adjust Business Hours**: Update hours in header, footer, and contact pages as needed
6. **Modify Services**: Add, remove, or modify service options based on your actual offerings
