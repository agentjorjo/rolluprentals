Storage Container Rentals

Roll Up Rentals provides secure, weatherproof storage container solutions throughout Los Angeles County. With 13+ years of experience serving contractors, businesses, and residents, we understand the critical need for secure, accessible storage on job sites and for various storage needs.

Our storage containers are perfect for construction equipment, tools, materials, inventory storage, seasonal items, and temporary or long-term storage solutions. All units are delivered to your location, placed exactly where you need them, and picked up when you're done.

Storage Container Options

20-Foot Storage Container

20' L × 8' W × 8.5' H
  • Approximately 1,170 cubic feet of storage space
  • Standard height - ideal for most equipment and materials
  • Corten steel construction - weather resistant and durable
  • Double door design for easy access
  • Lockable with your own padlock or our high-security locks
  • Forklift pockets for easy positioning
  • Ventilation to prevent condensation
  • Perfect for: Construction sites, retail overflow, event storage

40-Foot Storage Container

40' L × 8' W × 8.5' H
  • Approximately 2,390 cubic feet of storage space
  • Double the capacity of 20-foot units
  • Ideal for large equipment, bulk materials, or inventory storage
  • Same durable Corten steel construction
  • Double door ends for access from either side
  • Multiple locking points for enhanced security
  • Can be partitioned for separate storage areas
  • Perfect for: Large construction projects, wholesale storage, disaster relief

  • Same weatherproof, secure construction
  • Popular with contractors needing vertical storage space

40' L × 8' W × 9.5' H
