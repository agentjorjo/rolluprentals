About Roll Up Rentals

Serving Los Angeles County with reliable equipment rental solutions for over 13 years

Our Story

Founded in 2011, Roll Up Rentals began as a small dumpster rental service serving local contractors in the Los Angeles area. Over the past 13 years, we've grown to become a comprehensive equipment rental company, providing dumpster rentals, storage containers, and mobile offices to businesses throughout LA County.

Our commitment to reliable service, transparent pricing, and customer satisfaction has earned us a reputation as one of the most trusted equipment rental providers in the region.

To provide Los Angeles contractors and businesses with reliable, affordable equipment rental solutions that help them complete their projects on time and on budget.
